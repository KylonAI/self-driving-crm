# Goals interview

Run this before building anything. Two short cards, not one long form. Recommend an option in each question's description. Prefill only what the person has already told you.

## Card 1: the business

1. **What the CRM is for.** B2B sales, Partnerships, VC / investing, Recruiting, Something else. Sets object names and the pipeline.
2. **What you sell.**
   - A product people sign up for (self-serve or trial). Product analytics ties usage to each account, and the daily sign-up digest applies.
   - A service or offline sale, where the website is the lead source. Analytics covers website visits and lead source only.
   - Both.
3. **Partners.** Do partners, resellers or referrers live in the same CRM as customers (tagged as partners), or in a separate App? Recommend the same CRM with a partner tag unless partners have their own pipeline, commission or portal.
4. **Who works customers.** Pick the people (workspace member picker) and, for each, their role: account owner, meeting host, support, partner manager. These are the people who connect their own mailbox and calendar in step 4.
5. **Your own email domains.** For example `yourcompany.com`. Used to tell outside parties from teammates.

## Card 2: the tools

Ask which they use today. Checkbox groups, with the built-in Other answer for anything not listed.

- **Current CRM:** HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Attio, Close, Zoho, a spreadsheet, none.
- **Email:** Google Workspace (Gmail), Microsoft 365 (Outlook). Each person can use a different one.
- **Calendar:** Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar.
- **Booking page:** Cal.com, Calendly, HubSpot Meetings, Google appointment schedule, none.
- **Website forms:** form on our own site, HubSpot forms, Typeform, Tally, Google Forms, Webflow forms, none.
- **Meeting note taker:** Read AI, Fireflies, Otter, Fathom, Granola, tl;dv, Zoom AI Companion, Google Meet notes, none.
- **Analytics:** PostHog, Microsoft Clarity, Google Analytics, none.
- **Billing:** Stripe, other, none.
- **Support:** Intercom, Zendesk, a shared inbox, none.

Some tools can be reached either directly or through the mailbox. For example, a scheduler or note taker the team does not want to connect still sends confirmation and recap emails, and the Gmail or Outlook sync can recognise those senders. Offer that path when someone would rather not connect a tool.

## After the interview

- Save a one-page summary file in this Room: business type, what they sell, partners decision, people and roles, team domains, tools per category, and the order of steps you will run.
- Read back the plan in one message: the steps that apply, in order, and who has to connect what. Ask for a go.
- Skip steps that do not apply. A services business with no product skips the sign-up digest; a team with no note taker skips step 6 except for the follow-up draft written from calendar events.
